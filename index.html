Daily Ganbatte

Daily Ganbatte is a YouTube Shorts series of gentle, handmade-looking clay animations: a Shiba puppy, a Maltese puppy and a kitten live small everyday moments and offer one soft reassurance.

The "Daily Ganbatte Pipeline" app is the channel owner's own tool. It uploads and schedules the channel's videos, adds captions and translated titles, posts one welcome comment per video, and reads the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to anyone else.
