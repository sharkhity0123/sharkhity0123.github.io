Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

The Daily Ganbatte Pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the Daily Ganbatte YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses YouTube's APIs to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, set their captions, translated titles and thumbnails, post one comment per video from the channel, read comments left on the channel's videos, and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics.

What it stores

It keeps video IDs, publication dates and aggregate analytics for the channel's own videos so it can plan future episodes. Viewer comments are read to find everyday moments viewers describe; no personal information about viewers is stored, sold or shared.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
